## 1.1.1

* Production release of `device_identity_plus`.
* Adds native persistent device unique identifier support:
  * Android: `Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID`.
  * iOS: `UIDevice.current.identifierForVendor` (IDFV).
* Zero third-party dependencies.
* Broadened Flutter and Dart SDK compatibility (`sdk: '>=3.0.0 <4.0.0'`, `flutter: '>=3.0.0'`).
* Full universal documentation and privacy compliance notes.
